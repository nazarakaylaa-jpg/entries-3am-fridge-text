A single line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am: it has counted four door-openings, zero snacks eaten, and it is done being a museum.

> 3am. You have opened me four times tonight and eaten nothing. I am not a museum. Pick something, or stop flirting with my light.

Run: `node main.ts` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
